# JestBox Contributor Authorization and Rights Certification

## 1. Project licensing

JestBox is proprietary software distributed under the repository's All Rights Reserved licence. Public repository visibility does not grant permission to use, copy, modify, adapt, publish or redistribute project materials.

The standard Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) 1.1 is designed for contributions submitted under an identified open-source licence. It is not, by itself, a proprietary software assignment or a substitute for a rights-holder-approved contribution agreement. JestBox therefore does not represent standard DCO 1.1 as its operative proprietary contribution instrument.

## 2. Authorization prerequisite

A proposed contribution is not authorized merely because a pull request can be opened. Before work is undertaken or accepted, the contributor must obtain express written authorization from the rights holder that identifies:
- the permitted contribution or task;
- the permitted repository and intended use;
- the applicable ownership or licence treatment;
- any third-party material restrictions;
- confidentiality and disclosure expectations, if applicable.

The rights holder may reject or revoke authorization to the extent permitted by applicable law and the written agreement.

## 3. Contributor certification

As a condition of submitting an authorized contribution, the contributor must confirm in writing that:
1. They created the contribution or have sufficient rights and authority to submit it.
2. The contribution does not knowingly infringe another party's intellectual-property or confidentiality rights.
3. Third-party code, data, fonts, images, text or other assets are identified with their applicable licence/permission and attribution requirements.
4. They have disclosed any employment, client, academic or third-party restrictions that may affect their ability to contribute.
5. They have not included credentials, personal data, confidential information, malware or unrelated proprietary material.
6. They understand that submission does not grant the public a licence to use or redistribute JestBox.

## 4. Rights and acceptance

The precise rights granted to JestBox, any assignment, contributor ownership retention, warranties and permitted sublicensing must be set out in the written authorization or separate signed agreement before the contribution is accepted. This policy alone does not transfer copyright or create an implied assignment.

Do not merge a contribution where the required written authorization and rights terms are missing or ambiguous.

## 5. Sign-off record

The project may record a contributor's certification in a pull-request comment or other retained written record, provided the record identifies the contribution and the applicable authorization. A Git commit trailer such as `Signed-off-by` is not, on its own, proof that a proprietary rights agreement exists.

## 6. DCO and OpenSSF assessment boundary

JestBox may evaluate DCO-style sign-off controls as part of its OpenSSF assessment, but it must distinguish:
- a contributor policy;
- a written authorization/rights agreement;
- a commit sign-off statement;
- automated validation of a sign-off trailer;
- actual enforcement as a required pull-request check.

These are separate controls. Do not state that DCO is enforced unless a validator is installed, required checks block non-compliant changes, and actual pull requests demonstrate the control. Do not claim that a DCO sign-off alone satisfies proprietary ownership or licensing requirements.
