# OpenSSF Programme Eligibility and Applicability — JestBox

**Review date:** 2026-11-28

## Project licensing and scope

JestBox is proprietary software under an All Rights Reserved licence. Its public GitHub visibility does not grant source reuse, modification, redistribution or sublicensing rights. The project is currently governed by a single maintainer.

## OSPS Baseline

The OSPS Baseline is published as an Open Source Project Security baseline. Its current version on the official site at the date of this review is v2026.08.28. Level 1 is described as applicable to projects with any number of maintainers/users; Level 2 eligibility includes at least two maintainers and a small number of consistent users. The Baseline is a point-in-time self-attestation and requires the exact version to be stated.

Because JestBox is proprietary, formal programme applicability must be confirmed before claiming OSPS Baseline compliance. The existence of repository controls may still be used as an internal security benchmark, but that is not equivalent to a formal OpenSSF attestation. Level 2 cannot be claimed while the stated maintainer eligibility condition is not met.

## OpenSSF Best Practices Badge

The Best Practices Badge programme is described by OpenSSF as a voluntary self-certification programme for FLOSS projects. JestBox's proprietary licence means it should not claim a Passing, Silver or Gold badge unless programme administrators confirm that the project is eligible despite its licence.

The Silver criteria include a legal mechanism for contributor authorization (DCO, CLA or another legal mechanism). JestBox's project-specific written authorization policy is designed to address contributor rights, but it is not a standard DCO and its legal sufficiency has not been independently reviewed.

Gold includes material contributor-diversity and bus-factor requirements. A single-maintainer project cannot truthfully claim those conditions.

## Permitted internal use

The registers in this repository may be used as internal gap-analysis checklists. They must be labelled internal, dated and evidence-based. Do not publish an award badge, claim a maturity level or submit self-attestation until eligibility, exact criteria and evidence are reviewed.
