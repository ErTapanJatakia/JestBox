## Our commitment

JestBox aims to be usable by people with different access needs. Accessibility is an ongoing design, engineering, and testing activity. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and evaluation target. The graphic above is a reference mark; it does not mean W3C or WAI has reviewed, approved, or certified JestBox.

## Accessibility features

Settings & Superpowers includes text sizing from 50% to 200%, extra contrast, reduced motion, underlined links, larger touch targets, increased line spacing, and stronger keyboard-focus outlines. Your choices are saved locally on your device. The app also provides ten colour themes, semantic page structure, labelled controls, keyboard navigation, visible focus, live announcements, responsive layouts, and support for operating-system reduced-motion and forced-colour preferences.

The language and category pickers support text search and keyboard navigation. The launch screen can be dismissed by touch or keyboard input. Sharing is initiated by the user through the platform's share interface.

## Conformance and testing status

These features are implementation measures, not proof that every screen or combination meets every WCAG success criterion. No independent audit or W3C certification is claimed. Keyboard-only use, screen-reader announcements, text enlargement, reflow, contrast, every theme, all six locales, dialogs, and Android behaviour require practical testing across supported devices and assistive technologies. Any unverified or failing criterion must be recorded and addressed before a conformance claim is made.

## Known limitations

- Browser, operating-system, and receiving-app share interfaces are controlled by their respective platforms.
- Assistive-technology behaviour can differ by browser, operating system, device, and screen reader.
- Android packaging and native integrations require separate device-level accessibility testing.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Please include the affected screen or control, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, device, operating system, browser, and assistive technology/version where relevant. Do not include sensitive personal information.
