## Our commitment

JestBox aims to support people with different access needs, including keyboard and screen-reader users and people who enlarge text. WCAG 2.2 Level AA is our design and evaluation reference. The graphic above is not W3C/WAI approval, review, or certification.

## Current accessibility measures

The interface includes semantic structure, skip navigation, labelled controls, visible focus, dynamic status announcements, responsive layout, larger-text and high-contrast options, reduced-motion support, and forced-colors styles. Theme, settings, and share dialogs are provided. These measures still require observed testing with keyboards, screen readers, browsers, and devices.

## Conformance status

The earlier maintainer statement related to a previous two-locale web build. It does not automatically cover this six-locale redesign, its dialogs, ten themes, or Android wrapper. The current build is undergoing a new accessibility evaluation. No independent audit or W3C certification is claimed. Before a conformance claim is made, all locales, themes, contrast combinations, zoom/reflow, keyboard paths, screen-reader announcements, reduced motion, forced colours, and Android behavior must be tested.

## Report an accessibility barrier

Include the affected screen/control, steps, expected and actual result, device/OS/browser, and assistive technology/version. Do not include sensitive personal information.
