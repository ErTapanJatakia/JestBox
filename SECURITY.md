# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately. **Do not** disclose an unpatched vulnerability in a public GitHub issue, discussion, pull request, social-media post, or other public channel.

JestBox accepts private vulnerability disclosures at any of the following addresses:

- **General security and privacy contact:** cyber.litigation.advisor@gmail.com
- **Russia:** ertapanjatakia@yandex.com
- **All other regions:** tapan_ditu_17.20@outlook.com

You may use whichever address is appropriate for your region or communication needs. If you are unsure, use the general security and privacy contact.

### What to include

Please provide, where practical:

- A concise description of the suspected vulnerability and its potential impact.
- The affected version, commit, file, component, browser, or environment.
- Clear reproduction steps and a minimal proof of concept.
- Any relevant logs or screenshots, after removing personal data, credentials, tokens, and other secrets.
- Your preferred contact method for follow-up.

Please do not include real users' personal information, live credentials, private keys, or unrelated sensitive data. Do not access, modify, disrupt, or exfiltrate data belonging to other people. Do not conduct destructive testing or denial-of-service activity.

### Coordinated disclosure

Please allow the maintainer a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address a report before public disclosure. The maintainer will make reasonable efforts to acknowledge reports, assess their validity and impact, and communicate next steps. No fixed response or remediation SLA is currently offered.

Please do not publish details while a report is being investigated unless coordinated disclosure has been agreed with the maintainer. Good-faith research that follows this policy is welcome.

## Scope

This policy covers the JestBox source code and the browser-based application maintained in this repository, including its English (en-US) and Russian (ru-RU) experiences.

Third-party services, browsers, hosting-provider infrastructure, user devices, and unrelated repositories are outside the maintainer's direct control. Please report vulnerabilities in those products to their respective security teams as well.

Android packages and app-store distributions are not currently identified as supported release channels by this policy. This section will be updated if those distribution channels are introduced.

## Supported Versions

JestBox is currently under development; there is no stable, generally released version with a published support window at this time.

| Version / channel | Security updates |
|---|---|
| Current development branch | Reviewed and addressed as part of ongoing development, as appropriate |
| Earlier commits and unreleased snapshots | No separate support commitment |
| Future stable releases | Support policy to be announced with the release |

## Safe Harbor

The maintainer does not intend to pursue legal action against researchers for good-faith security work that:

- Follows this policy and applicable law.
- Avoids privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, and unauthorized access to other people's information.
- Stops promptly and reports the issue privately when sensitive data is encountered.
- Gives the maintainer a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.

This safe-harbor statement does not authorize activity that would otherwise be unlawful or override obligations owed to third parties.
