# JestBox Security

Security and privacy are important considerations in the development and maintenance of JestBox. We aim to protect users and address legitimate security concerns responsibly.

## Reporting a Security Issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please report it privately:

**Email:** cyber.litigation.advisor@gmail.com

Please include:
- A brief description of the issue and its potential impact
- The affected app version or component
- Steps to reproduce the issue, where practical
- Supporting evidence that does not contain other users' personal information

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities publicly before allowing a reasonable opportunity for review.

## Scope

This policy applies to the JestBox application and its Android package. Issues involving third-party platforms, operating systems, app stores, browsers, or other services should be reported to their respective providers.

## Responsible Research

Good-faith security research is welcome when conducted lawfully and without accessing other users' data, disrupting services, or causing damage. If you encounter sensitive information, stop testing and report the issue privately.

We will make reasonable efforts to review reports and address confirmed issues. Response and remediation times may vary depending on the nature and severity of the report.
