# JestBox Accessibility Statement

**Last reviewed:** 18 November 2026  
**Product:** JestBox  
**Languages:** `en-US`, `es-PE`, `ru-RU`, `es-ES`, `en-IN`, and `hi-IN`

## Our commitment

JestBox aims to be usable by people with different access needs, including keyboard users, screen-reader users, people who enlarge text, and people who use operating-system accessibility settings. Accessibility is an ongoing engineering and testing activity.

We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and evaluation target. The logo above is a conformance-mark graphic; it does not mean W3C/WAI has reviewed, tested, approved, or certified JestBox.

## Features in the current development interface

- Semantic header, main, footer, headings, and labelled sections.
- Skip navigation and visible keyboard focus.
- Searchable language and category inputs with associated labels and suggestions.
- Dialogs for themes, settings, and sharing.
- Ten theme palettes, with additional high-contrast and larger-text settings.
- Reduced-motion and forced-colors CSS support.
- Polite live regions for joke changes and action feedback.
- Responsive layout and touch-friendly controls.

These are implementation intentions and source-level features. Their usability must be confirmed with actual keyboard, screen-reader, browser, and device testing.

## Conformance status and scope

The previous owner-declared WCAG 2.2 Level AA statement related to the earlier two-locale browser build. It must **not** be treated as automatically covering this new six-locale redesign, the new dialogs, the theme palettes, or the Android wrapper. The current release candidate is under renewed accessibility evaluation. No independent audit or W3C certification is claimed.

Before asserting conformance for this release, the maintainer must evaluate all six locales, every theme, dialogs, text enlargement, contrast, reflow, keyboard-only operation, screen-reader announcements, reduced motion, forced colours, and the Android WebView/native share experience. Any failing or unverified success criterion must be documented and resolved or clearly scoped.

## Known limitations

- Browser and operating-system share dialogs are platform interfaces and are not fully controlled by JestBox.
- Assistive-technology behavior varies by browser, operating system, screen reader, and device.
- Android packaging requires a separate native accessibility review.
- This statement does not represent third-party certification.

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

Please report the affected screen/control, steps, expected and actual results, device/OS/browser, and assistive technology/version where relevant. Avoid including sensitive personal information.
