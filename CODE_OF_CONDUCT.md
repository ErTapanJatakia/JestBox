# JestBox Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

JestBox welcomes constructive participation in a respectful, safe, and
collaborative environment. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, project-related communications, and community spaces where
someone is representing the project.

## Expected behaviour

Participants are expected to:
- be respectful, considerate, and constructive;
- discuss ideas and technical decisions without attacking people;
- give and receive feedback professionally;
- respect differing backgrounds, experience levels, cultures, and languages;
- keep reports and private communications confidential;
- focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:
- harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory, hateful, or degrading language or conduct;
- sexual harassment or unwelcome sexual attention;
- doxxing or publishing another person's private information without consent;
- deliberate disruption, impersonation, or repeated bad-faith participation;
- sharing malicious code, malware, credentials, or harmful payloads unrelated
  to a legitimate and authorized project contribution;
- pressuring maintainers or contributors to disclose private information.

## Reporting a concern

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately using the
developer/support contact route published in the repository README. Please
include a concise description, relevant links or evidence, and whether you
believe there is an immediate safety concern. Avoid including unnecessary
personal or sensitive information.

Do not report security vulnerabilities in public issues or through a public
conduct report. Use the private vulnerability-disclosure instructions in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) once that channel is published.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information
may be shared only where needed to assess or respond to a report, protect
participants, or comply with applicable law. The project does not promise
absolute confidentiality or a particular response time.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this
Code of Conduct. Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a
warning, removal or editing of inappropriate content, temporary restriction
from project spaces, rejection of a contribution, or a permanent ban from
project participation.

The response will be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. The
maintainer may take immediate action where necessary to protect participants
or project systems.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual
is officially representing JestBox in public spaces.

## Attribution and changes

This is a project-specific policy. The maintainer may update it as the project
and its community needs evolve. The current version in the repository is the
authoritative version.
